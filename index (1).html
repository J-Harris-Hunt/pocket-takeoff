Your estimate

Standard counter practice
  • Depth: 25 1/2 in over standard 24 in base cabinets (1 to 1 1/2 in front overhang).
  • Seating overhang: about 12 in for counter height, 15 in for bar height, with support brackets.
  • Sink and cooktop cutouts are normally not deducted. Fabricators price the full area.
  • Draw a gap for a freestanding range (usually 30 in) and a dishwasher (24 in). There is no counter there.
  • Backsplash is commonly 4 in high. Full-height backsplash runs to the wall cabinets.
  • Stone is cut from slabs, so a fabricator may charge for more than your net area. Add 10 to 15 percent as a starting point.
  • Have a fabricator or installer template and confirm every dimension before you order.
